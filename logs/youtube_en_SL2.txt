TITLE: Why Australia Fought Birds with Machine Guns... and Lost | The Great Emu War

DESCRIPTION:
In 1932, the Australian military deployed heavy machine guns and 10,000 rounds of ammunition against an unexpected enemy: 20,000 emus ravaging wheat fields. What started as an official operation soon became one of history's most bizarre military failures. This is the true story of the Great Emu War.
