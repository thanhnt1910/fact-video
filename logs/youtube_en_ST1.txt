TITLE: Why Did This Soldier Fight for 29 Years After WW2 Ended? | Hiroo Onoda

DESCRIPTION:
In 1945, World War II ended, yet on the Philippine island of Lubang, Second Lieutenant Hiroo Onoda kept fighting for nearly 29 more years. Leaflets, family letters, and loudspeaker broadcasts were all dismissed as enemy traps. What kept him bound to the jungle for decades, and what was the true toll paid by the islanders?
